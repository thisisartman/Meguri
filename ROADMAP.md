# Roadmap

What's coming to Meguri, roughly in order. Each item has an issue where you can follow it or add your thoughts, and everything is on the [project board](https://github.com/users/thisisartman/projects/2). Plans change, so treat this as a direction rather than a promise.

## Now: 0.6, the first public test version

- **Backups to Google Drive and your own server.** Besides a folder on the phone, back up to Google Drive (through Android's own folder picker, with no Google sign-in in Meguri) or to your own WebDAV or Nextcloud server. ([#3](https://github.com/thisisartman/Meguri/issues/3))
- **A smoother catalogue for everyone.** Issue forms for adding places and collectibles, reporting availability and correcting details, with a simple confirmation step before changes go into the region data. ([#2](https://github.com/thisisartman/Meguri/issues/2))
- **Small fixes from 0.5.0.** Places you add sit under the right prefecture, and maps redraw properly after a map download. ([#8](https://github.com/thisisartman/Meguri/issues/8))

## Next

- **Suggest places from the app.** Add a new place or collectible, or report that something is sold out or has new hours, straight from its page in Meguri. Other collectors can confirm it, and confirmed changes arrive with the next "Update all". ([#1](https://github.com/thisisartman/Meguri/issues/1))
- **More of Japan, prefecture by prefecture.** The catalogue grows in waves: first finishing Niigata, Saitama, Tokyo and Gunma, then the rest of JR East's area (Kanagawa, Chiba, Ibaraki, Tochigi, Nagano), then Tohoku, then the rest of Japan in the order people ask for it. Within each prefecture, nationwide lists (manhole cards, dam cards, roadside stations) go in all at once, station stamps line by line, and goshuin and other local collectibles area by area and city by city, with collectors' help. The full plan is in [Growing the catalogue](docs/CATALOGUE_PLAN.md). ([#5](https://github.com/thisisartman/Meguri/issues/5))
- **Categories and types.** Separating *where* you collect (stations, shrines and temples, mountains, dams, roadside stations) from *what* you collect (stamps, goshuin, cards, tags), so a station with both a stamp and an Ekitag, or a shrine with a goshuin and a stamp, reads naturally. ([#4](https://github.com/thisisartman/Meguri/issues/4))

## Later

- **A friendlier app guide.** A short "first ten minutes" and plainer words. ([#9](https://github.com/thisisartman/Meguri/issues/9))

- **A visual changelog.** A page for each version showing what changed, with before and after pictures. ([#6](https://github.com/thisisartman/Meguri/issues/6))
- **Two-way trip planning with Google Maps.** Send a day's plan to Google Maps and bring routes back into Meguri. ([#7](https://github.com/thisisartman/Meguri/issues/7))

## Have an idea?

Open a [feature request](https://github.com/thisisartman/Meguri/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml). Ideas that come up often, or that make collecting on the go easier, move up the list.
