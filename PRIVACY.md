# Privacy

Meguri doesn't have accounts or servers. Your finds, photos and settings stay on your phone, and in any backups you decide to save. Nothing is collected about you: there are no analytics, no ads, and no crash reports sent anywhere.

You can read the same information in the app, under Settings › Help and about › About › Privacy.

## When Meguri goes online

Meguri only connects to the internet when you use a feature that needs it, and only to these services:

| Service | When | What it receives |
|---|---|---|
| [OpenFreeMap](https://openfreemap.org/privacy/) | Showing map tiles outside the offline maps you've downloaded | The area of the map you're looking at |
| [Nominatim (OpenStreetMap)](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy) | Searching for a place when you add or edit an item, or when you paste a Google Maps link | The name you searched for, and the area around you (about 50 km) so nearby places come first |
| [GitHub](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) | Opening Regions or downloading one | The list of regions and the files you choose, from [meguri-data](https://github.com/thisisartman/meguri-data) |
| [Eki kara Manhole](https://ekikaramanhole.whitebeach.org/) | Downloading card pictures | The codes of the cards |
| [Google Maps](https://policies.google.com/privacy) | Getting directions, or pasting a Google Maps link into Trip | The place you open (a pasted link is opened once to see where it leads) |
| [Google Play services](https://policies.google.com/privacy) | Your position for alerts, and the card scanner | This is handled on your phone by Play services |

As with any website, these services can see your IP address when Meguri connects to them.

## Sending feedback

"Send feedback" (Settings › Help and about) opens your own mail app with a message ready to send to the developer. You can see everything before it goes: your note, any screenshots you picked, the app version, your Android version and phone model, and the app's log. If you'd rather not send the log, untick "Include the app log". Nothing is sent until you press Send in your mail app.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why Meguri asks for it |
|---|---|
| Location (while you use the app, or all the time if you turn on alerts) | To show distances and what's nearby, and to alert you when you pass something you don't have yet |
| Notifications | For those alerts |
| Running in the background and starting when the phone starts | To keep alerts working when the app is closed or the phone restarts (only while alerts are on) |
| Photos and photo location (optional) | To add photos from your gallery, and to see where a photo was taken |
| Internet and network state | For the services above (network state lets the map know whether you're online) |

## Backups

Backups are files saved to a folder you choose. They hold your finds and, if you like, your photos. Card pictures are never included.
