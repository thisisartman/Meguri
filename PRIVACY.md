# Privacy

Meguri has no account and no server. Your finds, photos and settings stay on your phone, and in the backups you choose to save. Nothing is collected about you: no analytics, no ads, no crash reporting sent anywhere.

The same text is in the app: Settings › Help and about › About › Privacy.

## When Meguri goes online

Only when you use the feature listed, and only to these services:

| Service | When | What it receives |
|---|---|---|
| [OpenFreeMap](https://openfreemap.org/privacy/) | Map tiles outside the offline maps you downloaded | The area you look at |
| [Nominatim (OpenStreetMap)](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy) | Place search, when adding or editing an item and for places in a pasted Google Maps link | The name, and the area around your position (about 50 km) so nearby places come first |
| [GitHub](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) | Region downloads, when you open Regions or download one | The list of regions and the files you choose, from [meguri-data](https://github.com/thisisartman/meguri-data) |
| [Eki kara Manhole](https://ekikaramanhole.whitebeach.org/) | Card pictures, when you tap Download | The card codes |
| [Google Maps](https://policies.google.com/privacy) | Directions, and a Google Maps link pasted into Trip | The place you open; a pasted link is opened once to see where it leads |
| [Google Play services](https://policies.google.com/privacy) | Your position for alerts, and the card scanner | Handled on your phone by Play services |

Like any server, these see your IP address when Meguri connects.

## Feedback

"Send feedback" (Settings › Help and about) opens your own mail app with a message to the developer. You see everything before it is sent: your note, the screenshots you picked, the app version and phone model, and the app's log only if you tick it. Nothing is sent unless you press Send in your mail app.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Location (while in use; all the time only if you turn on alerts) | Distances, Nearby, and alerts when you pass something you still need |
| Notifications | Those alerts |
| Photos (optional) | Adding photos from your gallery, and reading where a shared photo was taken |
| Internet | The services above |

## Backups

Backups are files you save to a folder you choose. They contain your finds and, if you choose, your photos. Card pictures are never put in backups.
